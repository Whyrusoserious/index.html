About me
01 / 06

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. I'm Judy, a creative soul who finds joy in the spaces between music, visual art, and gaming.

I used to write for a magazine, I draw fan art, I photograph every moment, and I lose myself in games like Onmyoji and Animal Crossing.

This site is my journal, my gallery, my fav music - all in one place. Make yourself at home.

Writing is the place where I go when everything else feels too loud. I spent years contributing to a magazine in Vietnam - features, essays, the occasional short story - learning how to find the right word when words are all you have.

Now I'm working on something longer. A novel. I hope it finds its way to you soon.
